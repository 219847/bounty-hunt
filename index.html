The 5 parts you'll complete

Finish all five and you earn the SHSM red seal. Pick a piece below to see what it involves.

Credit Bundle

A set of tech-related courses — like computer science, communications tech, or design — that you take as part of your normal course load. Same credits, sharper focus.

Certifications

Industry-style certificates that prove you can use real tools and follow real standards. They're worth listing on a resume or a college application.

Sector-Partnered Experience

A hands-on project or placement with an actual tech partner, so you see how the skills you're learning get used outside the classroom.

Reach Ahead

A structured look at what's next — college programs, university options, or apprenticeships — so Grade 12 doesn't feel like a guessing game.

Experiential Learning

Workshops, field trips, and hands-on sessions that put tech in your hands early — including the trip that gets the most excitement: Ubisoft Toronto.

Your capstone: one project, your call

In Grade 11 or 12, you'll build one major project with mentorship. Pick a type to see what it could look like.

Build an app

Design and build something people would actually open — a study helper, a habit tracker, a school event app.

What you learn

  • App design and basic user experience
  • Coding logic and problem-solving
  • Testing something real people use

Build a website

A site for a club, a cause, or your own portfolio — live, and yours to show off.

What you learn

  • HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript
  • Layout, accessibility, and design
  • Publishing something to the real web

Design a game

A small puzzle, platformer, or strategy game — built, tested, and playable by your classmates.

What you learn

  • Game logic and interaction design
  • Level and mechanic design
  • Player feedback and iteration

Produce a media project

A video, a digital art series, or interactive media about something you actually care about.

What you learn

  • Storytelling and visual design
  • Editing and media tools
  • Sharing work with a real audience

Build a hardware project

Sensors, small electronics, or a simple robot — something that exists in the physical world, not just on a screen.

What you learn

  • Basic circuits and wiring
  • Coding paired with physical devices
  • Troubleshooting something real

A taste of the journey

Here's roughly how it unfolds, year to year.

  1. 1

    Discover your interest

    You join ICT SHSM and start your credit bundle — the courses feel like your regular timetable, just aimed at tech you actually like.

  2. 2

    Earn your certifications

    Along the way, you pick up industry-style certificates — proof you can use real tools, not just talk about them.

  3. 3

    Get mentorship

    Through your Sector-Partnered Experience, you work alongside people in the tech industry — not just your teachers.

  4. 4

    Build your capstone

    You take on a personal passion project — an app, a game, a media piece, whatever fits — with mentors guiding you along the way.

  5. 5

    Go on a tech field trip

    Experiential learning includes trips like a visit to Ubisoft Toronto — seeing a real studio, not just reading about one.

  6. 6

    Celebrate the milestones

    Some moments deserve more than a checkmark — like your Capstone Completion Experience and the Grade 12 Year-End Celebration.
