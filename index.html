ICT SHSM Courses

ICD2O – Grade 10 Digital Technology and Innovations

Computer number systems, innovations, and coding in Python. Strongly recommended but not required for ICS3U.

ICS3U – Grade 11 Computer Science

Introduction to programming, problem solving, algorithms, and software development. Usually in Java.

ICS4U – Grade 12 Computer Science

Advanced programming concepts, data structures, OOP, and software design. Usually in Java.

TEJ3M – Grade 11 Computer Technology

Computer hardware, networking, operating systems, and electronics fundamentals.

TEJ4M – Grade 12 Computer Technology

Advanced networking, PC repair, system configuration, and IT infrastructure.

TGI3M – Grade 11 Interactive New Media & Animation

Digital animation, interactive media, motion graphics, and creative design tools.

TGI4M – Grade 12 Interactive New Media & Animation

Advanced animation, digital effects, multimedia production, and creative workflows.

TGV3M – Grade 11 TV, Video & Media Production

Video editing, camera operation, audio production, and media storytelling.

TGV4M – Grade 12 TV, Video & Media Production

Advanced video production, studio workflows, broadcasting, and digital media creation.

Co-op (COOP2) – ICT Sector

Two-credit co‑op placement in an ICT field such as programming, IT support, media, or networking.


All Grade 12 ICT courses may be used as one of 6 courses for university admissions.
